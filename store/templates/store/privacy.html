Privacy Policy

Your privacy matters to us. Here is how MAPSCART collects, uses, and protects your personal details when you browse or order from our store.

Brand: MAPSCART (Curated in Delhi, India) Last Updated: November 2026

1. Information We Collect

When you browse MAPSCART, create an account, or place an order, we only collect the details needed to fulfil your order and provide customer support:

  • Contact & Delivery Details: Your full name, email address, mobile number, and shipping address (flat/house number, street/area, landmark, city, state, and PIN code).
  • Order & Payment Verification Details: Products ordered, applied offers, and any payment confirmation screenshot you voluntarily upload for UPI/Paytm QR order verification.
  • Account & Session Data: Essential cookies and session identifiers used to keep you signed in, preserve your shopping cart, and save your wishlist.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To process, pack, and ship your jewellery orders accurately across India.
  • To verify UPI/Paytm QR payments and share order/tracking updates via email or WhatsApp.
  • To respond to your questions, styling inquiries, or support requests submitted through our Contact page or WhatsApp concierge.
  • To prevent fraud, protect site security, and comply with applicable Indian laws.

3. Data Protection & Third-Party Sharing

We never sell or rent your personal information to third parties. We only share necessary delivery information (such as your name, address, and phone number) with trusted courier/logistics partners strictly to deliver your parcel, and use secure cloud infrastructure for hosting and media storage.

4. Cookies & Session Storage

MAPSCART uses essential cookies and browser session storage to remember your cart items, login session, and wishlist preferences. You can clear or disable cookies in your browser settings at any time, though some cart/checkout features require essential cookies to work properly.

5. Your Rights

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your account and personal order contact data at any time by contacting our support team.
