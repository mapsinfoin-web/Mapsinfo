Jewellery Care & Longevity Tips

To preserve the brilliant 18K gold gleam for years, follow these simple golden rules:

  • The "Last On, First Off" Rule: Put on your jewellery *after* applying makeup, body lotions, and perfumes.
  • Wipe Clean: Gently wipe with a soft dry cotton or microfiber cloth after a long day to remove skin oils.
  • Store Dry: Store each piece separately in your provided MAPSCART velvet box or airtight pouch away from direct sun and humidity.
